Project Detail

Aris Mining DCF Valuation

An intrinsic-value discounted cash flow model for Aris Mining Corporation (NYSE/TSX: ARIS), built from audited filings and company-specific operating drivers, and delivered as a reproducible Python model, a formula-driven Excel workbook and an executable notebook.

Headline Result

Valuation date 5 August 2026. Reporting currency USD. Base-case intrinsic value of $14.65 per share against a market price of $15.91 — roughly 7.9% downside, so the conclusion is approximately fairly valued. The market sits at the 55th percentile of the low-to-high range.

The dominant uncertainty is the long-run gold price: a ±$600/oz move in the long-run anchor swings value by $10.80 per share, roughly five times the next largest driver. This is a gold price call wearing a company's clothes.

What Makes the Model Company-Specific

  • Segovia's dual mining model is preserved. Owner Mining is guided on AISC per ounce; the Contract Mining Partners (~33% of mill feed) are guided on an AISC sales margin percentage, so their cost per ounce is price × (1 − margin) and rises with gold. Blending the two into one dollar cost would materially overstate gold leverage.
  • The projection is driven off AISC, the metric management actually guides. The identity cost of sales = AISC + by-product revenue − social contributions − sustaining capital reconciles to the audited FY2025 figure exactly (472.768 + 20.019 − 27.446 − 50.835 = 414.506).
  • Finite mine life, no perpetuity. Production is capped by mineable inventory; Segovia depletes in 2037 and Marmato in 2041. Depreciation is units-of-production and drives the asset base to exactly zero as the orebody is exhausted. A Gordon-growth value is reported only as a cross-check (it would add $4.76 per share).
  • Growth projects are options, not assets. Soto Norte and Toroparu are excluded from the core DCF and valued separately at WACC + 2%, then probability-weighted (40% / 35%). Both unrisked and risked numbers are disclosed.
  • Debt-like items beyond borrowings are deducted — lease liabilities, $200.8m of contract liabilities (gold prepay and stream obligations) and the $34.8m reclamation provision.

Methodology

Open Methodology

A) Historical Financials

  1. Pull audited FY2022–FY2025 statements from SEC XBRL companyfacts, CIK 0001964504, under the ifrs-full taxonomy — Aris tags its 40-F under IFRS, not US GAAP.
  2. Parse H1 2026 interims, operating KPIs and guidance from the 6-K HTML exhibits; the 2026 interims carry no XBRL facts at all.
  3. Validate units, dates and NaN propagation before anything downstream runs.

B) Operating Model

  1. Model production per stream (Segovia Owner Mining, Contract Mining Partners, Marmato) against mineable inventory rather than a growth rate.
  2. Build a real gold price deck fading to a long-run anchor of $3,300/oz in 2030.
  3. Unit costs: Owner Mining on AISC per ounce; CMP on an AISC sales margin, so cost/oz = price × (1 − margin).
  4. Depletion is units-of-production, driving the depletable asset base to exactly zero at end of mine life.

C) Pro Forma Statements

  1. Integrated income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement with explicit roll-forwards for cash, debt and PP&E.
  2. The contract liability unwinds as non-cash revenue in the cash flow statement.
  3. Balance sheet identity is asserted every projected year, not assumed.

D) Discounting and the Equity Bridge

  1. FCFF = NOPAT + D&A − sustaining capex − growth capex − ΔNWC, discounted at WACC with mid-year convention.
  2. No perpetuity: value ends when the orebody does. Gordon growth is reported only as a cross-check (+$4.76 per share).
  3. Growth projects valued separately at WACC + 2% and probability-weighted (Soto Norte 40%, Toroparu 35%).
  4. Bridge to equity by deducting borrowings, leases, contract liabilities and reclamation provisions, and adding cash.

E) Scenarios and Sensitivity

  1. Low / base / high flex linked drivers together (gold, production, costs, ramp timing, capital, tax, beta) so each case is internally coherent.
  2. Three two-dimensional grids plus a one-at-a-time driver tornado isolate what actually matters.

F) Verification

Every assumption is labelled fact (audited filing), guidance (published management guidance) or analyst (modeller's judgement), so a reviewer can sort by basis and see immediately which numbers are opinion. Failures are printed, never suppressed.

Data Sources

  • Audited financials FY2022–FY2025: SEC XBRL companyfacts, CIK 0001964504, ifrs-full taxonomy
  • FY2025 annual report, AIF and MD&A: Form 40-F filed 2026-03-11
  • H1 2026 interims, operating KPIs and guidance: Form 6-K filed 2026-07-29
  • Risk-free rate and inflation: FRED DGS10, DGS20, DFII10, T10YIE, CPIAUCSL
  • Price, share count, beta, gold: Yahoo Finance (ARIS, GC=F) — market observables only, never reported financial statements
  • Equity and country risk premia: Damodaran datasets

Every external datum carries a source id, URL, observation date, units and any transformation applied: source_register.csv. Every assumption is tagged by basis in assumptions_ledger.csv.

Known Limitations

  • Segovia's reserve life is five years. The model credits resource conversion to reach twelve. That is judgement, worth $3.75 per share, and is run both ways.
  • Marmato's cost target is unproven. Unit costs must fall from ~$2,542/oz to ~$1,150/oz, and first gold is only expected Q4 2026.
  • The WACC assumes constant target gearing while the assets deplete. Borrowings are held flat then amortised to zero; the tension is inherent to applying a perpetual capital structure to a finite asset.
  • Colombia's gold surtax is not modelled explicitly. The 35% rate may understate the marginal rate at high gold prices; the low case uses 38%.
  • Growth-project economics are analyst estimates. Capital and production for Soto Norte and Toroparu are not taken from a published feasibility study.

Reproducing It

  • pip install -r requirements.txt, then cd aris && python main.py rebuilds every artefact: CSVs, charts, the Excel workbook and the reconciliation.
  • python main.py --offline rebuilds from preserved raw data with no network access and must match the online run exactly.
  • pytest tests/ -v runs 78 tests, no network required.
  • outputs/aris_dcf_model.xlsx is formula-driven on the valuation path across 24 tabs — change the WACC or share count and the answer moves.
